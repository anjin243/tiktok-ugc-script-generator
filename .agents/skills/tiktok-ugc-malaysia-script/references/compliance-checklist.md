# Compliance checklist

## Product truth

- Use only supplied facts or explicit evidence.
- Do not infer material, size, quantity, accessories, origin, durability, or performance from an image.
- Preserve real color, SKU, structure, packaging, and included parts in every prompt.
- If AI output visibly differs from the product, label it unusable for direct publication.

## Restricted claims

Require evidence before using UPF50+, complete waterproofing, medical/therapeutic effects, UV-blocking percentages, polarization certification, fire/cut resistance, safety guarantees, test results, or official certification. Avoid unverifiable superlatives such as best, number one, guaranteed, totally safe, permanent, and 100% effective.

## Brand and intellectual property

- If a logo/brand appears, state that authorization must be checked.
- When unclear, write `品牌授权待确认` and `Kebenaran jenama perlu disahkan`.
- Do not present an unbranded product as a known brand or imitate protected packaging, logos, characters, music, celebrity likenesses, or competitor creatives.
- Do not assume a marketplace listing proves authorization.

## Pricing, privacy, and services

- Use price, discount, voucher, gift, stock, and deadline only when supplied and currently valid; otherwise say `semak harga/promosi semasa`.
- Do not expose API keys, account/buyer/store data, or personal information.
- Do not upload data or call paid APIs without explicit authorization.

## Pre-publish review

Verify spoken/on-screen claims, evidence, scene duration, Malay meaning, Chinese translation, promotion validity, authorization, music/media rights, product consistency, and current platform rules. State that the skill creates scripts/prompts only, not finished media.
