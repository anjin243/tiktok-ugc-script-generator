# TikTok Shop Malaysia UGC Script

Create a script package, not a finished image or video. Keep every product fact traceable to user-provided information or evidence.

## Inputs

Require a product name, category, at least one verified selling point, a target customer or use scenario, and video duration (default 15 seconds). Optionally accept material, dimensions, colors/SKUs, price/promotion, style, output language, product-image notes, logo presence, brand authorization, and performance-test evidence.

Treat images as descriptive evidence only. Never infer invisible materials, certifications, performance, dimensions, included quantities, or authorization from appearance.

## Missing information

List missing or risky information before the script. Use conservative wording for ordinary gaps and label assumptions. Ask a blocking question only when a usable draft requires choosing between materially different facts.

Never add UPF50+, complete waterproofing, medical effects, a specific UV-blocking percentage, polarization certification, flame/cut resistance, test results, absolute safety, "best", "number one", or "100% effective" without explicit evidence.

## Workflow

1. Separate verified facts, unverified claims, prohibited inventions, and missing details.
2. Read [references/compliance-checklist.md](references/compliance-checklist.md) and apply every relevant check.
3. Read [references/malay-copy-guide.md](references/malay-copy-guide.md) before writing Malay. Use Malaysian Bahasa Melayu, not rigid translation or Indonesian defaults.
4. Select one supportable core angle.
5. Build a 15- or 20-second timeline unless another duration is supplied. Make durations add up exactly.
6. Draft Malay voiceover/captions, followed immediately by faithful Chinese translations.
7. Draft image and image-to-video prompts that preserve the real product's color, quantity, material, dimensions, accessories, logo, and visible structure.
8. Mark unclear branded authorization as `品牌授权待确认` / `Kebenaran jenama perlu disahkan`.
9. Fill every section in [references/output-template.md](references/output-template.md) in order.
10. Run the final self-check.

## Do not use this skill

Do not use it to generate or claim delivery of final media, determine legal compliance conclusively, impersonate a brand, invent reviews/test evidence, alter product identity, or upload product/store/user data. Do not call paid APIs unless the user separately authorizes provider, cost, and data transfer.

## Product and brand integrity

- Prompt for authorization review whenever a logo or brand is present; never assume sale rights.
- Never turn an unbranded item into a known-brand item.
- Never change product color, quantity, accessories, material, dimensions, packaging, or important structure.
- Warn that visibly inconsistent AI imagery must not be published as a real-product depiction.
- Never fabricate certificates, endorsements, or functions.

## Final self-check

Confirm all 20 sections are present; durations equal the requested total; claims are supported or qualified; Malay is natural and paired with Chinese; the first three seconds have a supportable hook; CTA does not invent scarcity/price/guarantees; authorization/evidence gaps are disclosed; prompts preserve the real product; and no finished media, paid call, upload, or legal approval is claimed.

Use [examples/example-outdoor-hat.md](examples/example-outdoor-hat.md) and [examples/example-sunglasses.md](examples/example-sunglasses.md) as reference cases, never as fact sources for another product.
